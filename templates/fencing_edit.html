    Fencing Topology

    Define which fencing devices are used for each target. Targets can be node names, patterns, or node attribute/value pairs.

    Index specifies a fencing level from 1 to 9. Lower indices are attempted first. Devices within a level are used in the listed order; all must succeed for that level to succeed.

    Enter device resource IDs separated by commas or spaces. Moving a row changes its display order, not its index.
